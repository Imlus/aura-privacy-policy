Privacy Policy for Aura — Evolution Merge

Last updated: 24 August 2026

Aura — Evolution Merge (“Aura”, “the game”) is published by Saulius Petrauskas (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the game handles data.

Who this game is for

Aura is a casual strategy game intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not designed or marketed for children under 13.

Data stored on your device

Aura stores game progress locally on your device. This can include scores, progression, game settings, language preference, equipped boosts, and an in-progress game state. We do not operate an Aura account system or an Aura cloud-save service, and we do not receive this local save data.

Google Play Games

If Google Play Games is available and you are signed in, Aura may use it to submit leaderboard scores and achievement progress. Google processes the related Google Play Games account and service data under its own policies. Aura does not request your email address and does not create an Aura account.

Optional rewarded advertising

Aura may offer an optional rewarded advertisement. Watching an ad is never required to play the core game. Ads are provided by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). When ads are requested, Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, ad/device identifiers, interaction information, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Before production ads are enabled, the game will present Google’s consent choices where required. You will be able to revisit applicable choices through the game’s Privacy choices setting. Google explains its advertising and privacy practices in its Privacy Policy and advertising technologies information.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. The third-party services described above may process data necessary to provide their services. Local Aura game data stays on your device until it is removed through the game, app, or device controls. Google retains data it processes according to its own policies.

Security

Aura stores game progress on your device and does not operate servers that collect or store your game-save data. Google Mobile Ads documents that its SDK encrypts collected data in transit.

Your choices and contact

You may use Android and Google account controls to manage applicable advertising and Google Play Games settings. To ask a privacy question or exercise an applicable data-protection right concerning Aura, contact: rengleris@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if Aura’s data practices change. The current version will always be available at this page.
